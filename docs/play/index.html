How to play

Professor Gearhart's robots have taken over Steel City. Find the coloured keys, shoot crates and cracked blocks open, and reach the exit. Stand on crates to keep out of a robot's way.

On a phone or tablet, use the buttons on the screen. The title has buttons for the high scores and both editors, and the editors have buttons for their keys: Rub out, Fill, Pick colour and Scroll change what a touch on the picture or the map does. A gamepad works too: A jumps, X or B fires, Start plays.
