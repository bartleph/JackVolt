How to play

Professor Gearhart's robots have taken over Steel City. Find the coloured keys, shoot crates and cracked blocks open, and reach the exit. Stand on crates to keep out of a robot's way.

On a phone or tablet, use the buttons on the screen and tap to play. A gamepad works too: A jumps, X or B fires, Start plays. The editors need a keyboard and mouse.
